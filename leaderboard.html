Not all metrics improve. Qwen 3B GRT reports mean throughput 1.67264 fps versus 1.74994 for its all-patch control (about 4.42% lower), even though its Open MOS, Token F1 and patch reuse improve. Route31 mean request time is essentially unchanged versus its all-patch control. Throughput is the mean of per-request sampled-frame rates, not total frames divided by total campaign time, and these single historical runs do not establish repeated speedup. Patch ratios measure patch projection only, not end-to-end FLOPs.
